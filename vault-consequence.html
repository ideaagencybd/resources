What Would You Do?

7,387 words 15 entries From book

Fifteen decisions with their downstream consequences.

Every decision has consequences. Most decisions have consequences you did not anticipate — some worse than you hoped, some better, some sideways. The purpose of this section is to make the invisible consequence chain visible.

For each scenario below: read the situation, decide what you would do BEFORE reading the outcomes, then read what happens with each option. The point is not to be right. The point is to see the fork clearly.
