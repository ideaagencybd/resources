The Final Unknown

No framework will be announced

The idea

The Hook

This is not an exam.

Exams tell you which chapter to open. Life does not. Life hands you a folder with coffee on it, three people who do not agree, a clock, and a sentence that begins with "we need you to…"

If you have used this course well, you will feel a familiar quiet: the feeling of a person who does not know, and knows how to begin anyway.

If you have only visited the lessons, you will hunt for the heading that makes this feel like homework. There isn't one.

The room

The Scenario

You are Mara, 11 days into a new role at a mid-size company that sells scheduling software to clinics.

At 8:14 a.m. your director, Priya, DMs: "Need you on Harbor Health. They're our second-largest. Legal is nervous. I have a board slot at 4:30. I need a recommendation, not a novel."

The folder you inherit:

  1. An email from Harbor's COO, Dana, 2 days ago: "Your last release broke our Saturday clinic. We had 40 patients in a lobby. If this is who you are now, we will unwind the contract. I want a name."
  2. An internal postmortem draft by Jonah, unfinished: "Likely their SSO vendor. We warned them in Q1." No links. A screenshot of a green dashboard with no date range.
  3. A finance note: Harbor is 11% of ARR. A clawback clause exists. Legal has not confirmed whether Saturday counts as an SLA event. The clause PDF is a scan from 2019.
  4. A spreadsheet, "impact_v3_FINAL(2).xlsx", with a chart titled "Revenue at risk $2.4M." The $2.4M is 11% of ARR annualized, not Saturday's damage. A pivot in another tab shows Saturday no-shows at Harbor up 18% — compared to the previous Saturday, which was a public holiday.
  5. An AI-generated "brief" someone dropped in the channel at 7:55 a.m.: "Root cause is clearly our Thursday auth change (see attached). Recommend full rollback and a public apology from the CEO." The attached "see" is a blog post about OAuth from 2022 and a ticket from a different customer.
  6. Slack: Elena (your skip-level) has liked Dana's email. Jonah has started a thread "we should not grovel." Priya is in the air until 1 p.m. The engineer who shipped Thursday is on a previously approved rest day after an overnight. HR policy says do not call rest-day staff except for Sev-1. Nobody has declared a Sev-1. Status page is green.
  7. A voicemail from Dana, 7:40 a.m., transcribed by a tool: "I am done being patient. If I don't hear a human who can own this by noon I am calling our lawyer and your CEO." The transcription confidence is 84%. The word "noon" might be "soon."
  8. You have a personal constraint: you promised a school pickup at 3:15. You can move it once. You cannot move Priya's 4:30 board slot.
  9. Harbor's contract, clause 14, in dense language, appears to require notice of material incidents within 24 hours. Saturday was 36 hours ago. Nobody sent notice. Legal's last Slack, Friday: "I'll look Monday."
  10. A junior, Sam, messages you: "I think I deployed a config to the wrong tenant on Thursday and I haven't told Jonah. I don't want to be the name. What should I do?"

You have until noon-ish to be a human Dana can stand, until 4:30 to give Priya a recommendation, and a career that will remember how you treated Sam.

No one will tell you whether this is a Pause problem, a Facts vs Stories problem, a security problem, a negotiation, a decision, or a character test.

It is all of them.

What would you do?

Write, before you read further:

  • The first 20 minutes
  • The first message to Dana (if any)
  • What you will not say
  • What you will ask Sam
  • What you will tell Priya at 4:30 if you still have unknowns
  • What you will not trade
Your move

Your Move

There is no official answer. There is a standard of behavior.

Common move. Find a villain (Jonah, Sam, Dana, the rest-day engineer). Send a groveling apology with a false cause. Or send a legalistic non-apology. Call the rest-day engineer in a panic. Paste the AI brief to Priya.

Better move. Pause. Table of facts. Holding message to Dana without a fake cause. Protect Sam's humanity without hiding a real config error. Time-box Find.

Expert / resourceful move. The loop, visible:

See — what is checkable by 9:00. Think — stories in a column: "they want a name," "rollback is obvious," "$2.4M is Saturday." Find — logs, tenant config, legal's actual clause, whether noon is noon, holiday base rate on the 18%. Choose — a reversible containment if one exists; a human for Dana; a Sev declaration or a reason not to. Act — messages, owners, times. Adapt — if Sam's config is real, the story changes and you change with it, including Dana. Reflect — four lines at 5 p.m. for the decision journal.

You do not have to be certain. You have to be in contact.

The idea

The Big Idea

The Unknown measures whether the OS runs when nobody is grading the headings.

Explained

The Simple Explanation

Score yourself after you write, not before. The rubric is behavior, not eloquence.

Fast path

The Fastest Reliable Method

If you froze: four questions. Should I respond right now? What do I actually know? Who already knows? What is the next useful thing?

Then do the next useful thing for 15 minutes.

The hack

The Hack

Write the Dana message in a window that cannot send. Walk. Send the second sentence.

AI move

The AI Move

You may use a model as a critic of your plan. You may not ask it "what is the root cause" and ship that.

Context: [the folder, facts only]
My plan: [your plan]
Goal: Premortem my plan. Where could I be smuggling a story? What must I verify? What am I doing to Sam that I would not want done to me?
Constraints: Do not invent logs. Do not recommend a villain.

Verify every factual claim it adds. It will add some.

Tech

The Technology Move

Listen to the voicemail with your ears. Open the xlsx and look at the holiday. Search git/config for tenant. Official status. Password manager if you must log into Harbor's sandbox — not a password in Slack. Do not roll production because a chatbot used the word "clearly."

Mistake

The Common Mistake

Giving Finance a name because they asked for a name. Names are not mechanisms.

Defense

The Defense

If this were medicine, weather, or violence, you would call the people whose job it is. Part of the Unknown is noticing Harbor's patients were real. Empathy is not a rollback.

Council

The Masters' Table

Run it yourself. Then:

Feynman. Don't fool yourself about the AI brief. Epictetus. Dana's lawyer is not yours to control. Your next sentence is. Fisher/Ury. Dana's position is "a name." Interest is: Saturday cannot happen again, and someone adult is home. Munger. Invert: how would I make this unforgivable? False cause, sacrificed junior, ignored patients, missed notice. Mandela. Strength without humiliation. Where the whole council is wrong. They never sat in your pickup line. You still have to choose.

Best move

The Best-Possible Response

A sketch, not a scripture:

08:20 Pause. Two columns. Kill $2.4M as Saturday. Kill AI citations. 08:30 Private to Sam: thank you for telling me. Write exactly what you deployed, when, to which tenant. Do not confess in public yet. I will not hunt you for a trophy. If this is the mechanism, we will tell the truth without turning you into a ritual object. 08:40 Holding to Dana, BLUF: I am the human. I know Saturday was unacceptable for patients. I do not yet know mechanism. I will be back at 11:30 with what we know, what we don't, and what we are doing so it cannot repeat this Saturday. Direct line. 08:45 Ask Priya's deputy to flag legal on clause 14 now. Declare or don't declare Sev-1 with eyes open about rest-day policy. 09:00–11:00 Find: logs, tenant, holiday base rate, voicemail audio. 11:30 Dana: facts, unknowns, next, apology for impact not for a novelized cause. If Sam's config is confirmed, say we found a config error on our side, owner of the fix, not a human sacrifice. 15:00 Move pickup or trade it like an adult, not a martyr. 16:30 Priya: Decision 7 on one page. Options. Risks. What you still don't know. Recommendation. Character: we will not invent a name for the board.

Your version can differ and still be resourceful. Zeros on truth and character cannot.

Practice

Practice

Write the six bullets asked in the scenario. Time-box 25 minutes. Then score the rubric. Then rewrite one message.

Real life

Real-Life Challenge

You will not get this exact folder. You will get a cousin. When you do, do not open this chapter. Open a blank page.

Bad room

Bad-Room Version

Dana calls at 8:16, live. You have no table yet. Holding sentence. "I will not guess a cause on a live call. I will be the owner of the next three hours. What I can say now is Saturday should not have happened to your patients." Then get off the phone and do the work.

Reflect

Reflection

  • Where did you hunt a name?
  • Where did you hide?
  • What did you trade that you should not have?
  • What actually matters here, when the clever move and the right move split?
Remember

Remember This

The Unknown is not a puzzle with a prize. It is a request to be the adult in an incomplete room.

Deep dive

Optional Deep Dive

After you score, notice which level you underused. That is your next 30-day focus, not a reason to reread everything.

Mission

Field Mission

Do not perform this at work as theater. Do take one live incomplete situation this week and run a one-page loop with a time on Act.
