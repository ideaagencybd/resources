Career-Path Scenario Planning

88 words

I'm 34 and 10 years into a career in [field]. My strengths are [list]. My constraints are [list — location, family, health, financial, etc.]. I'm considering three paths: (A) staying in my current role and deepening, (B) switching to [adjacent field], (C) starting something of my own in [specific area]. For each path, give me: the most likely trajectory over 5 years, the biggest risk, the biggest reward, and the specific move I should make in the next 90 days to preserve optionality on that path.
