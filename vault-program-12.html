A Quarter of Deliberate Practice

A Quarter of Deliberate Practice

2,868 words 12 entries From book

Twelve weekly briefings. One skill per week. One practice log, kept honestly.

The 30-Day Program in the main appendices got you started. This program takes you through a full quarter — the shortest realistic window in which sustained change compounds into visible capability.

Each week has three components:

  • A BRIEFING — the concept and its practical application, in 5 minutes of reading.
  • A PRACTICE ASSIGNMENT — the specific thing you will do this week.
  • A REFLECTION PROMPT — the specific question to sit with at week's end.

Do this program with a real notebook, paper or digital. The point is not to read the weeks. The point is to log the practice.
