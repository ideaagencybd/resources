A Quarter of Deliberate Practice

AI as a Genuine Collaborator

218 words

Anchored in: Level 6 (multiple lessons)

The Briefing

Week 11 makes AI use a professional discipline rather than a novelty. This week you use AI meaningfully every workday, using the CGCEO-V structure and verification protocol.

The measure at week's end is not 'did you use AI a lot' — it is 'did you produce work you couldn't have produced alone, and did you verify it before relying on it?'

You will also begin your personal prompt library — the artifact that becomes part of your professional edge over years.

This Week'S Practice

• Every workday this week: at least one substantive AI-assisted task with a full CGCEO-V prompt.

• For every consequential AI output: run the verification checklist before relying on it.

• Save at least 5 prompts that produced exceptional output. Label them by use case.

• One difficult conversation or decision this week: use AI as a red-team or role-play partner before acting.

End-Of-Week Reflection

— Which AI-assisted task delivered the biggest return relative to my solo version?

— Where did verification catch a mistake? What was the mistake?

— What is the workflow I most want to build into a personal template based on this week?

Remember This

“AI is a colleague on their first day. Give them context and check their work.”
