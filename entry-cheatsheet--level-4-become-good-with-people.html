Level 4 — Become Good with People

60 words

22. LMR: Listen, Mirror (3–5 words), Reflect (one sentence).

23. Point, Reason, Example, Point. Under pressure, PREP saves you.

24. The hard conversation today is cheaper than the same one next month.

25. SBI-I: Situation, Behavior, Impact, Inquiry into intent.

26. Five-column prep before negotiation. Silence after your ask.

27. Watch the eyes in the room. They reveal informal power.
