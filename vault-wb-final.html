THE CASE

The Final Unknown

346 words From workbook

You have joined a new organization and inherited a customer-facing process that is missing targets. The data is incomplete. Different stakeholders tell different stories. The manager wants improvement within two weeks. A supplier is unhappy. One spreadsheet contains inconsistent figures. A colleague says the problem is people; another says it is the system. Your usual software access is temporarily limited. An AI assistant is available, but the information you have may contain errors. You have two hours to produce a recommendation and a next-step plan.

Your Mission

  • Observe before diagnosing.
  • Define the actual problem.
  • Search and ask.
  • Use tools intelligently.
  • Generate options.
  • Check risks and second-order effects.
  • Choose and communicate.
  • Act on the smallest useful test.
  • Document assumptions.
  • Adapt when new evidence appears.

The Great Minds Intelligence Council — Final Session

Use the Council as a set of lenses, not as a source of magical answers. Do not impersonate historical thinkers or invent quotes. Apply their ideas conceptually and then test them against evidence and your values.

Socrates: What are we assuming?

Aristotle: What is the practical judgment required here?

Sun Tzu: What changes the strategic situation?

Epictetus: What is actually within control?

Machiavelli: What incentives and power relationships are operating?

Ibn Khaldun: What social forces and system dynamics matter?

Kahneman: Where could judgment be biased?

Herbert Simon: What does bounded rationality imply?

Shannon: Where is information being lost or distorted?

Feynman: Can the explanation be made simpler without becoming false?

Drucker: What actually matters?

Deming: What about the system is producing the behavior?

Munger: How could this fail?

Buffett: What compounds and what is the opportunity cost?

Tetlock: How confident should we be?

Taleb: What fragile assumption or tail risk are we ignoring?

Frankl: Does the decision preserve meaning?

Mandela: Can strength coexist with humanity?
