Satisficing

63 words

Herbert Simon's term for accepting the first option that meets your criteria, rather than continuing to search for the theoretically optimal one. Won him a Nobel Prize.

Example: Buying the second decent apartment you view, that meets your criteria, rather than viewing 40 more to find the theoretically best one.

See: Deep Card — Herbert Simon; Lesson 3.5 — Knowing When to Stop
