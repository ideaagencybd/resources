50 Ready-to-Use AI Prompts

3,424 words 50 entries From book

The starter kit for your personal prompt library.

Every prompt below follows the CGCEO-V structure (Context, Goal, Constraints, Examples, Output, Verification) from Level 6. Fill in the bracketed pieces with your specifics. Where a prompt reads a little verbose, that is deliberate — vagueness is the enemy of useful output.

These prompts work with any competent modern general AI (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or similar). For factual questions and current-state claims, prefer a search-grounded tool (Perplexity, ChatGPT with browse, Google's AI Mode).
