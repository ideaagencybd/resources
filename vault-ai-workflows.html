Not one-line prompts. Complete workflows — with human checkpoints.

A prompt is a single request. A workflow is a sequence of prompts with human review between them. Workflows are how AI stops being a novelty and becomes a genuine productivity multiplier.

Each workflow below is described as a sequence of steps: what AI does, what YOU verify, and how the two handoffs combine. These are the ones worth investing in — the ones that repay their setup time many times over.
