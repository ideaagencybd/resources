The Resourceful Human OS

321 words From book

The compact set of questions that runs underneath everything.

The following pages contain the entire operating system of a Resourceful Human, compressed into a set of questions you can ask yourself in any situation.

Print them. Fold them. Carry them. Read them once a week for a year. Notice which ones you skip.

WHEN……ASK
…confusedWhat do I actually know?
…stuckWhat don't I know? And who does?
…researchingWhere is the best evidence — and did I go there?
…overwhelmedWhat matters most in the next 90 minutes?
…emotionalShould I respond right now — or in an hour?
…someone is angryWhat is the underlying fear or need I am not addressing?
…making a decisionWhat happens next? And then?
…everyone agreesWhat are we all missing?
…someone disagreesWhat do they see that I don't?
…AI answersHow do I verify this?
…something failsWhat did reality just teach me?
…something succeedsWhy did this actually work?
…life is hardWhat is the next useful thing I can do?
…choosing between good optionsWhat actually matters here? Not to the world — to me.
…tired of tryingWhat is the smallest possible version of the right thing?
…someone attacksWhat is the calm version of the sentence I want to say?
…in a room I don't understandWho is the room really deferring to — and what do they know?
…considering a shortcutWho does this shortcut make me? And can I live with them?
…facing an ethical choiceWould I advise my child to do this? Would I be comfortable if it were public?
…looking back at 80Am I building the life I want to have looked back on?

You will not always have the right answer. You will often have the right question. The question is where the practice begins.

Remember This

“The OS is a set of questions. The practice is asking them.”

Nothing matches that filter.
