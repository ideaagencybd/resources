30 Thinkers Worth Sitting With · Modern Psychology & Decision Science

Daniel Kahneman

186 words

1934–2024, Israel / USA

Two systems. Trust one; question the other; know which is running.

What They Contributed

With Amos Tversky, Kahneman founded behavioral economics and the modern study of cognitive biases. His book Thinking, Fast and Slow synthesizes decades of research into the two-system framework: System 1 (fast, intuitive, pattern-matching) and System 2 (slow, deliberate, effortful). Most of our decisions run on System 1, which is efficient but systematically biased.

How To Apply It Now

For every lesson in Level 2 and Level 7: Kahneman's framework is the underlying theory. Learn the specific biases (Level 2.3). Notice when System 1 is running the show. Design decisions and processes so that System 2 engages when the stakes require it.

Where Their Thinking Breaks

Some specific findings from the heuristics-and-biases research have been contested or failed to replicate. The underlying framework has held up better than any particular study. Also: the framework can be applied too aggressively — many System-1 intuitions are actually correct in their domain (Gigerenzer's critique).

One Book Or Source

Thinking, Fast and Slow (2011). For a more critical companion, Gerd Gigerenzer's Gut Feelings.
