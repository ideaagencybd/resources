Case 8 — The Medical Bill You Can't Understand

106 words

Situation:

You receive a medical bill for thousands of dollars, with itemization you can't decode.

First move:

Do NOT pay it immediately. Request an itemized bill in plain-language form. Cross-check against your insurance's explanation of benefits (EOB). Contact your insurance about any discrepancy. Contact the provider's billing department about anything the insurance won't cover but seems unreasonable — hospitals frequently negotiate.

What can still go wrong:

Many medical bills contain errors. Many contain line items you can dispute. Some insurance denials are procedural and reverse when appealed. The 'just pay it to make it go away' impulse costs people thousands of dollars they didn't actually owe.
