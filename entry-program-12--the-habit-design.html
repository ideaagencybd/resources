A Quarter of Deliberate Practice

The Habit Design

201 words

Anchored in: Level 1.5

The Briefing

You've spent three weeks changing your response, your vocabulary, and your attention. This week: the underlying operating layer — one habit built properly, so it survives the weeks when you don't feel like it.

The rule that matters: design for your worst self, not your best. The 2-minute version. The existing trigger. The 'never miss twice.' These are not compromises — they are what makes habits actually stick.

This Week'S Practice

• Pick ONE habit to build this week — chosen from the practices you've been trying in weeks 1–3.

• Define its 2-minute version. Not the aspirational version.

• Identify the existing habit you'll stack it to.

• Track daily: did I do it (yes/no). Nothing more.

• If you miss a day: do NOT double up. Just do it the next day.

End-Of-Week Reflection

— What was the 2-minute version I chose, and did I hit it every day?

— Where did I feel resistance — and what was actually happening in those moments?

— What would the 4-week version of this habit look like? Am I ready to keep going?

Remember This

“Design for your worst self, not your best.”
