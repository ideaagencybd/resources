Scenario Planning for the Next 3 Years

92 words

For [industry / business area], generate four scenarios covering the next 3 years: (A) base case, (B) upside surprise, (C) downside shock, (D) an unlikely-but-important tail scenario. For each: (1) the top three drivers, (2) the early indicators to watch for, (3) the one strategic decision that would look most different in that scenario compared to others, (4) how likely YOU (the AI) would put this scenario, and where you are least confident.

Verify:

AI scenarios are pattern-recognition, not prediction. Use as prompts for your own strategic thinking rather than as forecasts.
