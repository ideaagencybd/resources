The idea

The Hook

You will not remember this course as a cloud of good intentions.

You will remember it as a handful of moves that showed up when your mouth was about to do something expensive.

This chapter is that handful, printed densely on purpose. It is not a substitute for the lessons. It is the index your future tired self can use in a hallway.

The room

The Scenario

You have ninety seconds before a door opens. You need a move, not a chapter. What do you look up?

Your move

Your Move

Circle ten. Only ten. The rest can live in the book.

The idea

The Big Idea

Retrieval beats possession. Forty is already almost too many. That is why each line is a move, a question, a mistake, and a recovery.

Explained

The Simple Explanation

How to use this sheet: read the move. Ask the question. Use the tool. If you have a model, use the AI move as a partner, not a mouth. When you fail, use the recovery.

Fast path

The Fastest Reliable Method

Front of a card:

  1. Should I respond right now?
  2. What do I actually know?
  3. What matters most?
  4. What is the next useful thing?
The hack

The Hack

Put the card in the place you become stupid: the laptop lid, the meeting notebook, the suitcase.

AI move

The AI Move

Context: I have the Resourceful Human cheat sheet. Situation: [facts].
Goal: Name the ONE tool I should use in the next five minutes and the first sentence.
Constraints: One tool. No stack of seven models.
Verification: What you might be misreading.
Tech

The Technology Move

A single note, not a second brain. Print it once. Paper survives empty batteries.

Mistake

The Common Mistake

Collecting the sheet and never failing in public with a tool.

Defense

The Defense

A cheat sheet cannot hold a marriage, a diagnosis, or a lawsuit. It can keep you from making those worse in the first ten minutes.

Council

The Masters' Table

Drucker. Contribution, not ritual. James Clear. The card is an environment. Feynman. If you cannot retrieve it, you do not have it. Where the council could be wrong. Over-reduction. Some days you need the whole lesson, not the line.

Best move

The Best-Possible Response

Door about to open. You read line 1 and line 9. You pause. You sort one fact from one story. You walk in.

Practice

Practice

Copy ten rows by hand. Handwriting is retrieval.

Real life

Real-Life Challenge

Use three lines in three domains this week: work, home, digital.

Bad room

Bad-Room Version
