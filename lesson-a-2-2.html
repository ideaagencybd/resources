See What's There

Observation before opinion

22 min5W1H843 words22 sections
Skill
Collect the scene before you interpret it
Tool
5W1H
Result
You notice what other people skip
In one line
Look longer than is socially comfortable. Then think.
The idea

The Hook

The room is full of people who have already decided what it is.

They decided on the walk over. They decided from the subject line. They decided from a face they have seen make that face before. By the time they sit down, they are no longer looking. They are confirming.

Seeing is a skill because the mind hates it. Seeing is slow, slightly humiliating, and it keeps you from being the first to sound smart. It is also the only way not to solve the wrong problem with great energy.

The room

The Scenario

A customer writes: "Your app is down."

Support has a green dashboard. Sales has a frightened account manager. Engineering has a theory about a region. You are on the bridge call. Everyone is already fixing their preferred object.

What, exactly, is down? For whom? Since when? Where? Who saw it? How did they see it?

If you cannot answer those, you do not have an incident. You have a mood.

Your move

Your Move

Common: Join a theory. "It's DNS." You might even be right. You are still guessing.

Better: "Can someone share a screenshot?" Better, still not a scene.

Expert: 5W1H on a shared note, filled in 4 minutes, with blanks left blank.

Resourceful: Expert, plus "what would a camera record" as a discipline — URL, timestamp, account, error text, not "it's down."

The idea

The Big Idea

Observation is not a personality. It is a checklist you run before your favorite story is allowed to speak.

Explained

The Simple Explanation

What happened — the event in boring nouns. When — clock time, timezone, duration. Where — system, place, team, URL, room. Who — observer, actor, affected. Why — last, and labeled as hypothesis until it isn't. How — mechanism or method of observation.

Journalists, incident commanders, doctors taking a history, detectives — they did not invent this because it is pretty. They invented it because memory and status destroy scenes.

Leonardo's notebooks are a cult object for a reason that has nothing to do with genius-as-magic: he drew what was there. You do not need to be him. You need to delay why until what has a body.

The tool

The Practical Tool

Name: 5W1H

  1. Write the six words down the page.
  2. Fill only what you can point at.
  3. Put a ? on the rest. A question mark is a fact about your knowledge.
  4. Assign a check to each ? or consciously drop it.
  5. Only then let why into the room, as a list of candidates.

Fast path

The Fastest Reliable Method

In a verbal mess, ask only: When did it start, and what did you see on the screen? Two questions beat a theory.

The hack

The Hack

Take a picture. Of the board, the error, the fridge, the contract clause. Future-you cannot argue with a timestamped image the way present-you argues with a feeling.

AI move

The AI Move

Context: Incident or misunderstanding. Raw notes:
[paste]
Goal: Fill 5W1H. Mark inferences as inferences. List the three cheapest observations still missing.
Constraints: Do not propose a root cause until What/When/Where/Who are non-empty.
Tech

The Technology Move

Logs, session replay, photos, maps, translation cameras, official status pages. Category: instruments that extend the eye. Not a replacement for going to look.

Mistake

The Common Mistake

Asking why first because it feels intelligent. Why is a story magnet.

Defense

The Defense

You can observe forever. The Enough Test (Level 3) still applies. Observation is the start of the loop, not a lifestyle.

Council

The Masters' Table

Leonardo. Through the lens of looking until the obvious becomes strange. Feynman. Guess after you have something to be wrong about. Deming. The data of the system, not the anecdote of the loudest. Where wrong. Some truths are not visual. Do not demand a photograph of a feeling.

Best move

The Best-Possible Response

On the bridge: you share a note with six lines. You refuse to say "it's DNS" until What/When/Where have numbers. You look like slower than Jonah. You are the reason the next hour is about a real URL.

Practice

Practice

Replay a misunderstanding. Fill 5W1H. Notice which W you never ask.

Real life

Real-Life Challenge

Travel. "The hotel is a scam." What/when/where/who before the review-bomb. Work. "The team dropped the ball." Who, which ball, which clock. Family. "You always walk in and start." When, what, how — then a smaller true sentence.

Bad room

Bad-Room Version

Everyone is shouting why. You put what on the whiteboard in letters large enough to be rude. Rude accuracy is still accuracy.

Reflect

Reflection

  • Which W do you skip?
  • Who in your life sees well?
  • When did a photo save you from a story?
Remember

Remember This

If you skipped What and When, your Why is entertainment.

Deep dive

Optional Deep Dive

Police and medicine have formalized observation because the cost of a bad scene is blood. Knowledge work pretends it is exempt. It is not. It just bleeds slower — in money, trust, and years.

Mission

Field Mission

In the next meeting, write 5W1H silently for the first ten minutes. Speak only from the filled lines.
