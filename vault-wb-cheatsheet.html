Field Cheat Sheet

438 words From workbook
  1. Pause before you prove you are right. — Use the 10-Second Pause.
  2. Turn vague work into the next physical action. — Ask: “What can I literally do next?”
  3. Never research without a stopping rule. — Define what evidence would be enough to act.
  4. When stuck, find someone who has already solved it. — Use the Expert Request.
  5. When everyone agrees, search for the missing downside. — Run a mini-premortem.
  6. When AI answers confidently, verify the important parts. — Claim → Source → Date → Cross-check.
  7. When a process fails repeatedly, stop blaming people first. — Map the system and incentives.
  8. When a conversation gets hot, slow the tempo. — Name the issue before solving it.
  9. In negotiation, protect the relationship and your alternative. — Know your BATNA.
  10. Make important work visible. — Document decisions, owners, dates, and assumptions.
  11. Make good behavior easy and bad behavior harder. — Change the environment, not just your willpower.
  12. Do the smallest useful version first. — Create a safe test before a big commitment.
  13. Separate what is true from what is useful. — Evidence first; interpretation second.
  14. Separate a bad outcome from a bad decision. — Judge process with the information available then.
  15. The best tool is the one you can verify and control. — Capability > brand.
  16. Never automate an unclear process. — Simplify before automating.
  17. If the downside is catastrophic, add a guardrail. — Use approval, backup, or human review.
  18. Ask “and then what?” before acting on attractive plans. — Second-order thinking.
  19. When confused, write down what you know. — Then list the unknowns.
  20. When overwhelmed, choose the next useful thing. — Shrink the horizon.
  21. When criticized, extract the signal before defending yourself. — Ask: “What part is useful?”
  22. When you need certainty, check whether certainty is actually available. — Use ranges and scenarios.
  23. When a mistake happens, close the loop. — Fix, document, notify, prevent recurrence.
  24. When two people disagree, ask what each sees that the other misses. — Use disagreement as information.
  25. When you have influence, increase clarity and reduce fear. — People execute better when they understand the why and the next step.
  26. Do not confuse complexity with sophistication. — Prefer one good model over five mediocre ones.
  27. The goal is not to avoid all failure. — It is to fail cheaply and learn quickly where possible.
  28. Use AI to expand thinking, not outsource judgment. — Ask it to challenge, compare, and expose uncertainty.
  29. Protect your recovery systems. — Back up accounts, documents, contacts, and critical information.
  30. A calm person can create options. — Regulation is operational capacity.
  31. Wisdom begins where optimization stops. — Ask what actually matters.
