30 Thinkers Worth Sitting With

6,806 words 38 entries From book

The full profiles of the Council members who shape this course.

Throughout this book, the Great Minds Intelligence Council has appeared to sharpen specific problems. In these pages, each member gets a longer treatment: who they were, what they contributed, how their ideas apply to your work today, and where their thinking has important limitations.

This is not a hall of heroes. It is a working reference. Come back to it. Argue with the entries. Add your own.

The ordering follows roughly chronological arrival in the tradition, grouped by domain — ancient wisdom, scientific reasoning, strategic thought, modern psychology, and contemporary practice.
