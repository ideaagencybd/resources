The Situations Library

51 scenarios that actually happen — losing a job in a room you were called into, being followed home, a fire, a collapse, a scam call in your mother’s voice, a friend who says they want to die. Each one gives you what is really going on, the ordered moves, the exact words to borrow, what makes it worse, and the line at which it stops being yours to handle alone.
