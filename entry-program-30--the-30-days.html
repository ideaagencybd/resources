30 Days to Make It Real

The 30 Days

441 words
DAYFOCUSTODAY'S SPECIFIC ACTION
1The PausePractice the 4-Breath Reset three times today, on genuine triggers.
2Name what you feelAt 12pm and 6pm, name the specific emotion. Not 'fine.'
3Attention auditLog every interruption in one 90-minute block.
4Sleep and lightGet 10 minutes of morning outdoor light. No screens after 10pm.
5The 2-minute habitPick one habit. Do only its 2-minute version. Nothing more.
6Diagnose avoidanceName the flavor of one thing you've been avoiding. Take one small step.
7Week 1 reviewWhat did I notice about my baseline? What changed?
8Define a real problemTake one thing on your task list. Write the 5-level problem statement.
9FSIO one messageTag every sentence in one important message you received.
10Catch a biasNotice one of your own biases in real time. Write it down.
11Ask one real questionIn one meeting, ask a question from the 7-question ladder.
12First principlesTake one industry belief you hold. Ask why. Investigate one assumption.
13Change your mindPublicly, honestly, on one small thing. 'In light of X, I've updated.'
14Week 2 reviewWhere is my thinking sharper? Where is it still sloppy?
1590-minute skill sprintPick one skill. Do 30/45/15.
16Research one topicSearch, compare, verify. Chase one claim back to primary source.
17Four-line askSend one well-designed help request today.
18CODA-RApply the sequence to one thing you're stuck on.
19Listen using LMROne real conversation. Mirror, reflect, wait.
20PREP a hard messageOne email you were going to write casually — do it in PREP.
21Week 3 reviewWhat did I learn that I couldn't have learned by reading?
22Sunday 30 for the weekSet your Top Three. Book them. Defend them.
23Build one checklistPick a task you botch. Retrospectively build its checklist.
24Five-part delegationDelegate one thing properly. Include reverse brief.
25Map one processDraw the current state of one process. Interview one person who runs it.
26Prompt librarySave 3 AI prompts that worked exceptionally well this month.
276 digital-defense movesComplete all six if not already done. Set aside 3 hours.
28Week 4 reviewWhat system am I now running that I wasn't running a month ago?
29Decision 7 on a real oneTake one open decision. Run all seven questions. Sleep on it.
30The 10-year auditAnswer: in 10 years, what will I wish I had done more of this year? What less? What is one 90-day change I will make as a result?
